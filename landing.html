FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you get started.

Is TaskFlow really free?

Yes. TaskFlow is completely free to use with unlimited tasks, no credit card required, and no hidden fees — ever.

Can I use it on my phone?

Absolutely. TaskFlow is fully responsive, so it works great on phones, tablets, and desktops right in your browser.

How does real-time sync work?

TaskFlow runs on Firebase, so every change is synced to the cloud instantly. Open it on any device and your tasks are always up to date.

Can my team use it together?

Yes! Create a shared workspace and invite your team members to collaborate on the same boards in real time.

How is my data protected?

Your account is protected with secure authentication, and your data is stored safely in the cloud. Only you and the people you share with can see your tasks.

Do I need to install anything?

Nope. Just create a free account in your browser and start managing tasks right away. There's nothing to download.
